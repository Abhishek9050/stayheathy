Key points to understand before starting.

Can I cancel anytime?

The offer page states that cancellation depends on the selected plan. Review the current checkout terms before completing your purchase, especially after the introductory trial.

Is everything included?

The membership offer describes full access to 1,500+ videos, programs and new content, regardless of the chosen billing period.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. Pilates&Friends presents the library as suitable for all levels, including absolute beginners. Start with gentle, clearly instructed sessions and progress at your own pace.

What equipment do I need?

The platform describes training with a mat and internet connection. Some workouts may use additional Pilates equipment, so check the individual session description when needed.

What happens after the €1 trial?

The introductory offer is described as a 14-day trial. Before purchasing, read the current checkout page carefully for the exact renewal price, billing date and cancellation conditions that apply to your selected plan.
